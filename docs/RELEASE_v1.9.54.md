# mSCIdblit v1.9.54

This release adds ten selective primary-literature route annotations focused on
inhibitory synaptic receptor organization, KCC2-associated functional
continuations, and PTPRT-linked neuroligin/neurexin adhesion. The evidence layer
now retains SHISA7–GABRA2–gephyrin, SHISA7–GABRA2–collybistin,
radixin–GABRA5–gephyrin, PX-RICS–GABARAP–gephyrin,
PACSIN1–GLRA1–collybistin, KCC2–β-PIX, KCC2–brain creatine kinase,
KCC2–TASK-3, DSCAM-CT–PTPRT–neuroligin and DSCAM-CT–PTPRT–neurexin routes.

Several routes in this batch begin with receptor-associated accessory or
intracellular scaffold evidence rather than a classical secreted ligand. Those
compartment boundaries are retained explicitly in the route records; they are
useful receptor-complex and intracellular-continuation evidence for mSCS, but
are not promoted to extracellular ligand binding, causal traversal or a
canonical signaling cascade. Transcription-factor and terminal target-gene
layers remain unassayed for this batch; no causal graph edges or confidence
scores are created.
