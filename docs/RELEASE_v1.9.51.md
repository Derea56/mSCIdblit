syndecan signaling, APP trafficking, tenascin-associated growth-factor and
matrix branches, integrin–PECAM1 adhesion, GPC3–CD81 membrane association and

and the noncatalytic or moonlighting contexts of the matrix and plasminogen
branches. Transcription-factor and terminal target-gene layers remain unassayed
for this batch; no causal graph edges or confidence scores are created.
