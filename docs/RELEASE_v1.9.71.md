# mSCIdblit v1.9.71

## Gap-driven primary-literature expansion

This release adds a bounded composite route for NT-3 signaling:

- NT-3 > TrkC/NTRK3 > PLCgamma1 > CREB > BCL2

The route joins existing NT-3/TrkC/PLCgamma1 graph evidence with primary
oligodendroglial studies showing NT-3-dependent CREB activation and direct
CREB regulation of the BCL2 promoter (PMID:8776670; PMID:10737596;
PMID:15140194). The receptor-proximal and transcriptional observations come
from complementary studies, so the PLCgamma1-to-CREB continuation remains an
evidence-layer plausibility link rather than a newly asserted graph edge.

The route preserves the oligodendrocyte comparator context and adds no causal
edges or database confidence scores.
