# mSCIdblit v1.9.92

## Gap-driven primary-literature expansion

This release adds one composite route annotation for a previously unresolved
WNT5A receptor-to-output gap:

- WNT5A > ROR2 > MAPK8/JNK > spinal-neuron death in demyelinating CNS

The route combines primary evidence for WNT5A-ROR2 binding and noncanonical
JNK activation (PMID:12839624) with independent mouse demyelination evidence
showing Wnt-Ror2 pathway inhibition suppresses JNK-associated spinal-neuron
death and Wnt5a overexpression aggravates neurodegeneration
(PMID:27550808; PMCID:PMC5064219). The developmental and demyelination
studies remain explicitly separate; no single-study causal chain, terminal TF,
target gene, or SCI-specific transfer is asserted.

No graph edges or confidence scores are added.
