# mSCIdblit v1.9.136

This release adds 37 source-linked primary-literature route annotations for previously unresolved downstream evidence. The additions cover receptor-proximal scaffold and trafficking outputs, bounded ligand–receptor–output observations, complement and clearance complexes, trans-synaptic organization, and extracellular matrix or pathway-complex evidence. Intracellular, transcription-factor, and target-gene layers remain explicitly unresolved where the source does not establish them.

## Added evidence groups

- Complement-regulatory CRIT/TOR–C2 interactions and lectin-pathway outputs.
- Neuroligin/neurexin, GABA-A/glycine-receptor/gephyrin, and KCC2-associated synaptic organization.
- P2X2/P2X7 cytoskeletal, trafficking, pore, and cell-function outputs.
- MBL/CALR-CD91, C4BP/LRP1, C1q/LRP1, and calreticulin/SCARF1 clearance outputs.
- uPAR/CR3, PlexinA2/NgR1, CD47/integrin, and contactin/PTP-alpha receptor-complex outputs.
- IGSF8/KIR3DL2 immune output and Activin-A remyelination output with receptor topology left open.
- Claudin, tenascin-C/fibronectin, syndecan/MADD-4, chordin/BMP7, and collectin-11/MASP complex evidence.
