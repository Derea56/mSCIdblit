# mSCIdblit v1.9.55

This release adds eleven selective primary-literature route annotations that
complete the currently eligible receptor-proximal continuation set. The
evidence layer now retains Adm–RAMP2–CRHR1, neuroligin-2–MAGI2–VIPR1,
salivary IGFBP-related protein–insulin–LRP2, NEGR1–OBCAM–LSAMP,
calreticulin–C-reactive protein–FCGR2A, contactin-3–APP–ATP5F1A,
P-cadherin–E-cadherin–DSG2, syndecan–MADD-4–syndecan, FGFR4–SDC2–FGF22,
vitronectin–PVR and RAC1–WAVE regulatory complex–ARP2/3 routes.

Some routes in this batch represent accessory, scaffold, adhesion or
actin-regulatory continuations rather than canonical extracellular ligand
signaling. Those compartment boundaries are retained explicitly in the route
records; the annotations provide evidence for plausible signaling or receptor
complex continuation in mSCS without asserting a causal chain. Transcription-
factor and terminal target-gene layers remain unassayed for this batch; no
causal graph edges or confidence scores are created.
