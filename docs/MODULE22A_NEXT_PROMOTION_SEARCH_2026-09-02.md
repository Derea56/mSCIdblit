## Outcome

One additional bounded link was supported:

- `M22A-HANDOFF-000213` (`Bmp8a BMPR1A_ACVR2A`) → `M22B-E000346`
  (`SMAD1/5/8 → ID1`). A rat granulosa-cell study reports BMP8A-induced
  SMAD1/5/8 activation and Id1 transcripts, while a complementary receptor
  reconstitution study identifies ALK3/BMPR1A with ACVR2A as a canonical
  BMP8A receptor combination. The link remains bounded to pathway-program
  reuse; it does not claim exact-pair Id1-promoter occupancy or SCI transfer.
