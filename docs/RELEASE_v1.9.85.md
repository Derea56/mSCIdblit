# mSCIdblit v1.9.85

## Gap-driven primary-literature expansion

This release adds one strict full evidence route:

- IL33 > IL1RL1/ST2:IL1RAP > ERK1/2 > STAT3 > IL10 gene transcription

Primary human macrophage-derived foam-cell experiments detected membrane ST2L,
showed IL-33-dependent ERK1/2 and STAT3 activation, demonstrated IL-10
promoter activation and STAT3-site dependence, and confirmed STAT3 binding at
the IL10 regulatory region (PMID:29099095; PMCID:PMC5704190).

The route remains an evidence-layer annotation: causal status is unasserted,
traversal is not causal, and no confidence score or SCI transfer is claimed.
The receptor-to-ERK continuation is represented as a composite route annotation;
no new graph edge is asserted.
