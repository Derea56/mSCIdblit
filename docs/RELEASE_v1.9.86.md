# mSCIdblit v1.9.86

## Gap-driven primary-literature expansion

This release adds two explicit IL34–CSF1R ligand–receptor–output routes:

- IL34 > CSF1R > IDE expression and soluble oligomeric amyloid-beta clearance
- IL34 > CSF1R > HMOX1/HO-1 expression and antioxidant output

Primary mouse microglial experiments support IL-34 activity in CSF1R-expressing
microglia, induction of IDE and HMOX1/HO-1, and associated amyloid-beta
clearance and oxidative-neurotoxicity outputs (PMID:21872563;
PMCID:PMC3181379).

These remain evidence-layer annotations: intracellular continuation, TF identity,
causal status, traversal, and SCI transfer are unasserted. No graph edges or
confidence scores are added.
