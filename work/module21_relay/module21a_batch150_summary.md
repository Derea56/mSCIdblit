## Evidence boundaries

The melanocortin and opioid relay candidates use processed POMC products—ACTH, alpha-/gamma-MSH, and beta-endorphin—as the active molecular species. They do not claim that intact POMC is uniformly the ligand, and the precursor/processing limitation is recorded per row. Direct ligand/receptor activation, receptor-proximal second-messenger relay, and downstream function are represented as separate semicolon-delimited evidence layers.

POSTN–ITGAV_ITGB3 is retained as downstream function only because the primary study used integrin blocking and FAK/migration perturbations rather than a purified binary POSTN–alphaVbeta3 binding assay. MRAP, RAMP2, and RAMP3 are retained as accessory/receptor-complex boundaries, not standalone POMC receptors. Pathway similarity, co-expression, generic GPCR coupling, and cognate-ligand evidence were not transferred to exact unresolved POMC pairs.
