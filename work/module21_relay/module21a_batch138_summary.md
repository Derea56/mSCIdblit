# Module 21A Batch 138 Summary

Batch 138 reviewed 25 exact priority-8 queue rows spanning galectin/LGI, LHB receptor candidates, inhibitory immune receptors, LIN7 scaffolds, hepatic lipase, LIPH/LPA signaling, and the LMAN1-MCFD2 cargo receptor complex.

## Outcomes

- 7 reviewed relay candidates: LGALS3-LAG3, LGALS8-ITGA3, LGI3-ADAM22, LGI3-FLOT1, LGI3-STX1A, LIN7C-KCNJ4, and LMAN1-MCFD2.
- 4 reviewed binding-only records: LGI2-ADAM22, LHB-TSHR weak cross-reactivity, LIN7C-ABCA1, and LIPC-LRP1, with directness and complex limitations recorded in-row.
- 3 reviewed function-only records: LIPH-LPAR1, LIPH-LPAR2, and LIPH-LPAR4. These are explicitly indirect LIPH -> LPA -> LPAR relays, not direct LIPH-receptor interactions.
- 11 unresolved exact-pair boundaries: the non-TSHR LHB candidates and LILRB4-LAIR1, LIN7C-HTR2C. Search boundaries were recorded with stable PubMed/Reactome URLs.
- One terminal TF candidate was retained: STAT1 for LGALS8-ITGA3, based on measured STAT1 and phospho-STAT1 changes; no TF was assigned from pathway analogy or expression-only evidence.

## Search and evidence boundaries

Searches covered exact-symbol queries across PubMed, PMC, publisher pages, Reactome, and BioGRID where a database record helped resolve a primary citation. Direct molecular association, receptor-complex/trafficking, and downstream functional evidence were kept distinct. LGI3-STX1A and LGI3-FLOT1 are intracellular trafficking associations rather than conventional extracellular ligand-receptor pairs. LIPH evidence supports enzymatic LPA production and LPAR signaling through the product LPA, not direct LIPH-LPAR binding. The LHB-TSHR row is weak heterologous binding by intact purified LH; isolated LHB was not tested.

Only the requested raw TSV and this summary were written. Shared Module 21A registers, Module 20A ledgers, the frozen manifest, and integration outputs were not modified.
