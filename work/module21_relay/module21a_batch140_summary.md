Positive evidence was kept distinct by layer. LY86/CD180 was recorded as direct RP105/MD-1 receptor-complex assembly with TLR4/LPS regulatory function; LYPD3/AGR2 as direct extracellular binding with an indirect GPI-anchored scaffold relay; LUM/ITGB1 as alpha2beta1 binding with FAK/Src-ERK outputs; MBL2/LRP1 as direct CD91 binding with collectin-mediated uptake; MDK/ITGA4_ITGB1 and MDK/ITGA6_ITGB1 as direct integrin-complex binding with paxillin-linked migration or neurite-outgrowth outputs; and MDK/LRP2 as lower-affinity binding without an LRP2-specific downstream endpoint. Canonical LTalpha, LRP1/LRP2 lactoferrin, LFA-1, collectin, AGE-RAGE, and cytokine/receptor-family comparator evidence was documented but not transferred to unsupported exact pairs. Searches covered PubMed, PMC, publisher pages, and Reactome; negative or absent evidence was recorded explicitly.
