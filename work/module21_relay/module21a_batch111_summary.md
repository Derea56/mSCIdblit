# Module 21A Batch 111 review summary

Batch 111 contains 25 exact priority-8 collagen-family rows. The review searched PubMed, PMC, publisher pages, and Reactome-oriented pathway context, prioritizing primary binding, receptor perturbation, and functional studies. Only exact ligand/receptor or explicitly matched receptor-complex evidence was retained; related collagen or integrin-family results were recorded as boundaries rather than transferred.

## Outcomes

- 3 reviewed relay candidates: native type-I collagen–CD36 for `Col1a1 Cd36` and `Col1a2 Cd36`, plus collagen XVIII/endostatin–alpha3beta1 for `Col18a1 Itga3`.
- 2 reviewed binding-only candidates: collagen XVIII/endostatin–alphaVbeta3 for `Col18a1 Itgb3` and collagen XVIII/endostatin–alphaVbeta5 for `Col18a1 Itgb5`.
- 1 reviewed function-only candidate: collagen XVIII/endostatin modulation of alpha2beta1-dependent endothelial adhesion to collagen-I for `Col18a1 Itga2_Itgb1`; the study does not demonstrate direct endostatin–alpha2beta1 binding.
- 19 reviewed unresolved cases, chiefly collagen XIX/XX/XXIV exact alpha-integrin assignments and collagen-I with CD93, FLT4, alpha5beta1, alphaIIbbeta3, or beta3.
- No terminal TF assignments. SMAD/TGF-beta results from the collagen XXIV beta3 study were outside the exact submitted rows and were not transferred.

## Key limitations and search boundaries

The strongest collagen XIX result was a processed F4 peptide binding alphaVbeta3 and alpha5beta1, not alpha1beta1, alpha2beta1, alpha10beta1, or alpha11beta1. The collagen XXIV primary study reported a beta3-subunit co-immunoprecipitation and TGF-beta/SMAD functional branch, but no alpha1beta1, alpha10beta1, or alpha11beta1 assay. Generic collagen-I/II/IV integrin literature was not used to infer receptor sharing for collagen XIX, XX, or XXIV. For collagen-I with CD36, the positive evidence is for native fibrillar type-I collagen; the separate COL1A1 and COL1A2 rows therefore retain a trimer/chain-resolution caveat.
