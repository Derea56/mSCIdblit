# Module 21A Batch 143 review summary

Batch 143 reviewed the 25 exact priority-8 queue rows spanning neurocan and neurofascin adhesion, NGF-associated receptor/scaffold branches, nidogen-integrin complexes, neuroligin-neurexin adhesion, bombesin-family receptors, neuromedin-S/U comparator receptors, and Nodal receptor complexes. - 13 reviewed receptor-proximal relay candidates.
- 2 reviewed functional-only records: NCAN-CDH2 system-level inhibition and NGF-TRPV1 sensitization.

## Boundary decisions

Direct molecular edges, receptor-complex/pathway edges, and functional outputs were kept separate. NCAN-CDH2 was not promoted to direct binding because the primary study identifies GalNAcPTase as the neurocan receptor while measuring inhibition of N-cadherin function. NGF-SORT1 and NGF-SORCS2 records retain the mature-NGF versus proNGF distinction. NID1-integrin records refer to alpha3beta1 or alphaVbeta3 receptor complexes, not isolated alpha or beta subunits. Neuroligin-neurexin records retain splice-isoform and NRXN2-resolution limits. BRS3 orphan-receptor pharmacology and canonical NMUR signaling were not transferred to unsupported NMB/NMS/NMU pairings.

Only the requested raw TSV and this summary were written; shared Module 21A registers, Module 20A ledgers, the frozen manifest, and integration outputs were not edited.
