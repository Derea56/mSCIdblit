# Cross-module path linkage batch 03: Notch→RBPJ/CSL→HES/HEY

This batch links JAG1 and DLL4 Notch candidates to the canonical NICD/RBPJ/CSL
complex and to HES1, HES5 and HEY1 outputs. Notch→STAT3 and Notch→IL6→STAT3
functional branches remain separate from canonical HES/HEY transcription.

## Batch result

- 6 linkage records were added.
- DLL4→NOTCH1→RBPJ/CSL→HEY1 is supported across the relay and terminal
  program layers, while the 20B DLL4-NOTCH1 candidate remains boundary-only.
- DLL4→HES1 is retained as a ligand-discriminated composite candidate; primary
  signaling-dynamics data favor HEY1/HEY-family output for sustained DLL4.
- JAG1→NOTCH1→HES5 has SCI functional support, but no separate 22A
  JAG1-specific HES5 target record was assigned.
- JAG1→NOTCH1→STAT3 and DLL4→NOTCH1→IL6→STAT3 remain functional pathways,
  not canonical direct HES/HEY edges.
- JAG1→NOTCH2→RBPJ reporter output is retained as pathway evidence without a
  terminal TF-to-target assignment.
- No Module 20A LR classification or frozen manifest was changed.

## Supplemental primary-literature search

Six supplemental records were added for intake review:

- DLL4 produces sustained Notch1 signaling with predominant Hey1/HeyL output,
  while DLL1 produces pulsatile Hes1 output (PMID 29398116).
- JAG1-positive progenitor transplantation increased Hes5 and recovery after
  contusive SCI (PMID 22452482).
- N1ICD/RBPJ ChIP-seq and transcriptomics support the canonical neural-stem-cell
  HES1/HES5 target layer (PMID 22232070).
- DLL4/Notch plus BMP9 induced HEY genes in primary human endothelial cells in
  an RBPJ-dependent assay (PMID 26471266).
- DLL4 preferentially activated NOTCH1 over NOTCH2 in receptor assays
  (PMID 30289388), and structural analysis resolved direct DLL4-NOTCH1 binding
  (PMID 25700513).

These records remain supplemental candidates and are not silently promoted into
Module 21A or 22A registers.
